# 🎵 Suno Package — "Share It Fair (With Freddy)" (Division Song)

Copy-paste kit for the **sung** track. Higgsfield can't sing (its audio model
is speech-only) and Suno isn't wired into this session, so drop the **Style**
into Suno's style box and the **Lyrics** into the lyrics box, generate, and lay
the audio over the silent/ambient video clips (see `video-shotlist-division.md`).

Same friendly Freddy voice + kids-choir feel as the counting and 2× songs, so
the whole series sounds like one show.

---

## 🎚️ Style prompt (paste into Suno's "Style of Music" box)

```
upbeat children's nursery rhyme, kids sing-along, bright and bouncy, playful
marimba and glockenspiel, ukulele, hand claps, cheerful kids choir on the hook,
gentle four-on-the-floor kick, major key, 104 BPM, warm and wholesome,
Cocomelon / Super Simple Songs vibe, squeaky-clean mix, super catchy
call-and-response, joyful
```

**Title:** `Share It Fair (With Freddy)`
**Voice tip:** friendly warm lead for Freddy; let a kids choir shout the
answers ("one each!", "two each!").

---

## 🎤 Lyrics — matches the 5-clip video (~50s)

Numbers spelled where it helps Suno sing them cleanly. Each verse lands on the
clip it belongs to.

```
[Intro]
(Ribbit!) I'm Freddy Frog with a basket of berries
Let's share 'em all fair — it's easy, it's merry!

[Chorus]
Share it, share it, fair and square
Same for everybody — everybody gets a share!

[Verse 1 - two divided by two]
Two little berries, two lily-pad plates
One on each — now isn't that great?
Two shared by two is one each, you see
Two divided by two is one — hooray, whee!

[Verse 2 - four divided by two]
Four little berries, two lily-pad plates
Two on each — go on and count your mates!
Four shared by two is two each, you see
Four divided by two is two — one, two, whee!

[Verse 3 - six divided by three]
Six little berries, three lily-pad plates
Two on each — everybody celebrates!
Six shared by three is two each, you see
Six divided by three is two — sharing's the key!

[Outro]
Everybody's happy, everybody's fair
That's how we divide — we share, share, share!
(Ribbit... share it fair!)
```

## 📥 How to use it in Suno

1. Go to **suno.com** → **Create** → toggle **Custom** mode.
2. Paste the **Style prompt** into the style box and the **Lyrics** into the
   lyrics box. Set the title.
3. Generate, pick the take you like, download the audio.
4. Drop it onto the assembled video (clips are silent/ambient on purpose so the
   song sits cleanly on top). Trim the intro/outro to fit the ~50s cut.

> Want a longer version? Add more shares as extra verses using the same pattern
> — e.g. `8 ÷ 2 = 4`, `9 ÷ 3 = 3`, `10 ÷ 2 = 5` (`ten shared by two is five each`).
