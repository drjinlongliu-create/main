# 🎵 Suno Package — "Count With Freddy the Frog"

Everything you need to generate the **sung** track in [Suno](https://suno.com).
Higgsfield can't sing (its audio model is speech-only) and Suno isn't wired into
this session, so this is a copy‑paste kit: drop the **Style** into Suno's style
box and the **Lyrics** into the lyrics box.

> **Length note:** one Suno generation maxes out around 4 min (v4) / ~8 min (v4.5).
> For the full ~10‑minute song, generate it in **two or three parts** (or use
> **Extend**) — the lyrics below are split into parts you can render back‑to‑back.
> There's also a tight **~3‑minute version** at the bottom that matches the
> Claymotion video being rendered, so audio and video line up.

---

## 🎚️ Style prompt (paste into Suno's "Style of Music" box)

```
upbeat children's nursery rhyme, kids sing-along, bright and bouncy, playful
marimba and glockenspiel, ukulele, hand claps, cheerful kids choir on the hook,
gentle four-on-the-floor kick, major key, 104 BPM, warm and wholesome,
Cocomelon / Super Simple Songs vibe, squeaky-clean mix, super catchy
call-and-response, joyful
```

**Optional variations to try:**
- Swap `marimba and glockenspiel` → `steel drums, tropical` for a summery feel.
- Add `reggae skank guitar` for a laid-back groove.
- For the sleepy outro, generate that section separately with:
  `soft lullaby, music box, slow, tender, whispered kids vocal, 70 BPM`.

**Title:** `Count With Freddy the Frog`
**Persona/voice tip:** in Suno, set a friendly warm lead vocal; the `[Chorus]`
lines are written for a kids choir to shout the numbers.

## 📥 How to use it in Suno

1. Go to **suno.com** → **Create** → toggle **Custom** mode.
2. Paste the **Style prompt** into the style box and a **Lyrics** section into
   the lyrics box. Set the title.
3. Generate. Pick the take you like, then **Extend** with Part 2, then Part 3
   (for the full ~10‑min song), or just use the **~3‑minute version** for the
   video.
4. Download the audio and drop it onto the Claymotion video (the clips are
   silent/ambient on purpose so your song sits cleanly on top).
