## 📈 Posting tips for virality
- **Pin a comment** asking a question kids answer with a parent: "Which friend
  is YOUR favorite — can you count to their number? 🐸"
- **Post the 1-minute counting section (1→10) as a Short/Reel** with the same
  thumbnail frame as the cover — it funnels to the full video.
- Publish at **6–8am local weekend time** (kids' peak co-viewing).
- Reuse `#CountWithFreddy` on every clip so the series links together.
