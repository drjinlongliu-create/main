# Count With Freddy the Frog — Full Lyrics 🐸

*Total runtime ≈ 10:00 · Suggested tempo: 100–108 BPM, bright and bouncy*

Legend:
**[HOOK]** = the sticky, repeat-after-me chorus · *(action)* = dance move ·
`CALL:` / `KIDS:` = call-and-response

---

## 1 · Wake-Up Hook  (0:00–0:40)

*(Freddy stretches on a lily pad as the sun comes up.)*

> Good morning, good morning, the pond is bright and green,
> I'm Freddy, I'm a froggy, and I love to count and lean!
> I hop, I hop, from pad to pad and back —
> and every friend I meet, I add them to my pack!

**[HOOK]**
> **Hop, hop, add-em-up — how many friends have we?**
> **Count 'em on your fingers, count along with me!**
> **One little jump and the number grows —**
> **that's how counting and adding goes!**

---

## 2 · Counting-Up Hop, 1 to 5  (0:40–2:10)

*(One finger up on "one," two on "two," and so on.)*

> **One!** *(hop)* One green lily pad, sitting in the sun.
> `CALL:` How many? `KIDS:` **One!** Our counting has begun!
>
> **Two!** *(hop-hop)* A snail comes creeping, slow and shy and blue.
> Now one plus one more friend — hey, that makes **two!**
>
> **Three!** *(hop, spin)* A dragonfly is buzzing 'round the tree.
> Two friends and one more — count 'em: **one, two, three!**
>
> **Four!** *(stomp, stomp)* A turtle by the reeds knocks upon the door.
> Three friends and one more friend — now the pond has **four!**
>
> **Five!** *(high five!)* A duckling takes a dive and swims up to arrive.
> Four friends and one more — give a great big **FIVE!**

**[HOOK]** *(everybody count on fingers: 1-2-3-4-5)*
> **Hop, hop, add-em-up — how many friends have we?**
> One, two, three, four, five — **that's FIVE**, you'll agree!
> **One little jump and the number grows —**
> **that's how counting and adding goes!**

## 3 · Counting-Up Hop, 6 to 10  (2:10–3:40)

*(Keep the fingers going — now use both hands.)*

> **Six!** *(wiggle)* A fish does a flippity kick-kick-kick.
> Five friends and one more friend — count 'em up to **six!**
>
> **Seven!** *(reach up)* A bird sings a song from up in bird-heaven.
> Six friends and one more — now we're up to **seven!**
>
> **Eight!** *(sway)* A beaver's building something, and it's looking great.
> Seven friends and one more — that's a busy **eight!**
>
> **Nine!** *(clap)* A firefly is glowing, oh the pond looks fine.
> Eight friends and one more friend — twinkle up to **nine!**
>
> **Ten!** *(big jump!)* A bunny hops on by and says hello again.
> Nine friends and one more — now we've got a **TEN!**

**[HOOK]** *(count both hands: 6-7-8-9-10)*
> **Hop, hop, add-em-up — how many friends have we?**
> Six, seven, eight, nine, ten — **that's TEN**, can you see?
> **One little jump and the number grows —**
> **that's how counting and adding goes!**

## 4 · The "One More" Song  (3:40–5:00)

*(The +1 idea, slow and clear. Hold up fingers, then pop up one more.)*

> When you have a number and you want a little more,
> just **add one** — pop a finger — and you'll even up the score!
>
> `CALL:` One plus one is… `KIDS:` **two!**
> `CALL:` Two plus one is… `KIDS:` **three!**
> `CALL:` Three plus one is… `KIDS:` **four!**
> `CALL:` Four plus one is… `KIDS:` **five!** — you're adding brilliantly!
>
> *(pick up the pace)*
> Five plus one is **six**, six plus one is **seven**,
> seven plus one is **eight**, and eight plus one — say **nine!**
> Nine plus one is **ten** — oh, the counting is divine!
>
> **[HOOK]** (soft, half-time)
> **Hop, hop, add-em-up — one more makes it grow,**
> **the number climbs by one, and now you know!**

## 5 · Two Friends Meet  (5:00–6:30)

*(Two groups of friends hop together. Show one group on the left hand,
one on the right, then push them together and count the total.)*

> Over here are some friends *(left hand)*, over there are a few *(right hand)*,
> push 'em all together — let's see what they do!
>
> **Two** *(left)* plus **three** *(right)* — hop 'em in a line…
> count 'em all together: one-two-three-four-**FIVE**! *(five fine friends of mine!)*
>
> **Four** *(left)* plus **one** *(right)* — bounce 'em by the shore…
> count 'em all together: one-two-three-four-**FIVE**! *(five friends at the door!)*
>
> **Three** *(left)* plus **three** *(right)* — a hoppity delight…
> count 'em all together: one-two-three-four-five-**SIX**! *(six friends, out of sight!)*
>
> **Five** *(left)* plus **five** *(right)* — the biggest hop of all…
> count 'em all together: **SIX-SEVEN-EIGHT-NINE-TEN**! *(ten friends, standing tall!)*
>
> **[HOOK]**
> **Hop, hop, add-em-up — two groups become one,**
> **push 'em all together, adding's so much fun!**

## 6 · The Ten-Frame Chant  (6:30–8:00)

*(Number bonds to 10. Hold up ten fingers; fold some down to show pairs.)*

> Ten little fingers, a wiggly little crew —
> which two numbers hide inside, and make a perfect **ten** for you?
>
> `CALL:` Nine and… `KIDS:` **one** makes ten!
> `CALL:` Eight and… `KIDS:` **two** makes ten!
> `CALL:` Seven and… `KIDS:` **three** makes ten!
> `CALL:` Six and… `KIDS:` **four** makes ten!
> `CALL:` Five and… `KIDS:` **five** makes ten — say it again!
>
> *(clap the pattern)*
> Ten, ten, a perfect ten,
> fold 'em down and count 'em up, then do it all again!
> Nine-and-one, eight-and-two, seven-and-three are true,
> six-and-four and five-and-five — that's the ten-frame crew!
>
> **[HOOK]**
> **Hop, hop, add-em-up — the friends that make a ten,**
> **learn 'em once, you've got 'em, then say 'em all again!**

## 7 · Add It All Up — Finale Rap  (8:00–9:20)

*(Big, confident, foot-stomping review. Bring back every friend.)*

> Yo — it's Freddy on the pad and I'm here to say,
> we counted all our friends in a hoppity way!
> The **lily pad**'s one, the **snail** makes two,
> the **dragonfly**'s three and the **turtle**'s four — woo!
> The **duckling**'s five, the **fish** is six,
> the **bird** flies seven with a kick-kick-kick!
> The **beaver**'s eight and the **firefly**'s nine,
> the **bunny** hops ten and we're feelin' fine!

> `CALL:` So how many friends came out to play?
> `KIDS:` **TEN!**
> `CALL:` And if two hop home, how many stay?
> `KIDS:` **EIGHT!**  *(ten take away two, you're doing great!)*
>
> Now stack up five and five once more —
> `CALL:` five plus five is… `KIDS:` **TEN!** — like it was before!
>
> **[HOOK]** (full volume, everybody!)
> **Hop, hop, add-em-up — how many friends have we?**
> **ONE-TWO-THREE-FOUR-FIVE-SIX-SEVEN-EIGHT-NINE-TEN — that's TEN, you see!**
> **One little jump and the number grows —**
> **that's how counting and adding goes!**

*(Wind down. Soft and sleepy, folding one finger down each line.)*

> Now the sun is setting, so we count back down to rest…
> **Ten** tired friends *(yawn)*, all cuddled in a nest.
> **Nine**… the bunny naps. **Eight**… the firefly dims.
> **Seven**… the bird tucks in. **Six**… the beaver swims.
> **Five**… the fish drifts off. **Four**… the duckling's done.
> **Three**… the turtle yawns. **Two**… the snail says none.
> **One** little lily pad, glowing in the moon —
> goodnight from Freddy Frog… we'll count with you soon!
>
> *(whisper)*
> **Hop, hop… add-em-up… sweet dreams, everyone. 🌙**

### The takeaways children practice
- **Rote counting** 1→10 (segments 2–3) and **backward** 10→1 (segment 8).
- **+1 / "one more"** as the engine of counting (segment 4).
- **Combining two groups** = addition (segment 5).
- **Number bonds to 10** (segment 6).
- **Review + a gentle subtraction peek** (segment 7).
