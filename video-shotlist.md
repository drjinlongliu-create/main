# 🎬 Video Shot List — Claymotion Highlight (~3 min, 9:16)

The animated highlight rendered with **Higgsfield** (`gemini_omni`, Claymotion
style preset). 18 clips × 10 seconds = **3:00**, vertical 9:16 for Shorts /
TikTok / Reels. Clips are **silent / ambient on purpose** — overlay the Suno
track (see [`suno-prompt.md`](suno-prompt.md)) on top.

**Mascot:** Freddy, a round green clay frog. **Recurring friends (one per
number):** 1 lily pad · 2 snail · 3 dragonfly · 4 turtle · 5 duckling · 6 fish ·
7 bird · 8 beaver · 9 firefly · 10 bunny.

## Production notes
- **Style key:** Claymotion preset (`1de0f39e-…`), attached to every clip so the
  look stays consistent.
- **Educational design:** every number shows both the **numeral** and the
  **countable quantity** of friends, so kids can count even if a digit renders
  loosely. Segments 12–14 visualize addition as *combining groups*.
- **Aspect:** 9:16 (native to the preset) — ideal for vertical/viral platforms.
- **Audio:** none baked in; add the Suno song in any editor. The 3‑min song
  version in `suno-prompt.md` is timed to this sequence.
