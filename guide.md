# Teacher & Parent Guide — Count With Freddy the Frog 🐸

A companion to [`lyrics.md`](lyrics.md). Everything here is optional — the song
works on its own — but these notes help you turn 10 minutes of singing into real
math learning.

**Ages:** 3–7 (pre-K through Grade 1)
**Runtime:** ≈10 minutes
**Skills:** counting 1–10, counting backward, "one more" (+1), combining groups
(addition), number bonds to 10, a first peek at "take away" (subtraction).

---

## What each segment teaches

| Segment | Math idea | What to do while it plays |
|---------|-----------|---------------------------|
| 1 · Wake-Up Hook | Set the routine | Learn the hook + the hopping move. |
| 2 · Count 1→5 | One-to-one counting | Hold up one finger per number. Touch each friend on screen. |
| 3 · Count 6→10 | Counting past five | Switch to the second hand at "six." |
| 4 · One More | +1 is how numbers grow | Show a number, then *pop up* one more finger. |
| 5 · Two Friends Meet | Combining two groups = addition | One group per hand; push hands together and count all. |
| 6 · Ten-Frame Chant | Number bonds to 10 | Ten fingers up; fold some down to show each pair. |
| 7 · Finale Rap | Review + first subtraction | Cheer the total; on "two hop home," fold two fingers down. |
| 8 · Goodnight Countdown | Counting backward 10→1 | Fold one finger down per line; end calm. |

---

## Three ways to play it

1. **Just watch & sing** — best for the first few times. Let kids absorb the hook
   and the pattern.
2. **Fingers up** — everyone shows the number on their hands. This turns abstract
   counting into something they can *see and feel*.
3. **Props** — ten toys, buttons, or pom-poms. Add one per new friend in segments
   2–3; make two groups in segment 5; build tens in segment 6.

---

## Quick questions to ask (pause the video)

- After segment 3: *"We have ten friends — can you count them all again?"*
- During segment 4: *"Six and one more is…?"*
- During segment 5: *"Two friends and three friends — how many together?"*
- During segment 6: *"Seven and how many more make ten?"*
- During segment 7: *"Ten friends, two hop home — how many stayed?"*

If a child isn't sure, **count it out together** with fingers or props. The goal is
confidence, not speed.

---

## Why this design helps kids learn (and rewatch)

- **Repetition with variation:** every verse has the same shape and a new friend —
  predictable enough to join in, fresh enough to hold attention.
- **Multi-sensory:** see the number, say the number, show it on fingers, do the
  move. More pathways = stronger memory.
- **Counting *and* adding together:** each "one more" hop shows that adding one is
  the same as counting up — the single most important early-math connection.
- **A calm landing:** the backward countdown doubles as review *and* a wind-down,
  which makes it a natural bedtime or transition song.

---

## Extend the learning (after the song)

- **Draw the pond:** have kids draw and count their own ten friends.
- **Hop it out:** place ten "lily pads" (paper plates) on the floor and physically
  hop from one to ten, then back down to one.
- **Snack math:** count ten crackers, eat two — "how many left?"
- **Make your own verse:** invent friend #11 and #12 for kids ready to count past ten.

Have fun — sing it, hop it, add it up! 🐸➕
